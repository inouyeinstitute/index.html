(Elizabeth) Ann Takasaki

(Elizabeth) Ann Takasaki is a third generation Japanese American born in Richfield, Utah. She graduated from Brigham Young University with degrees in Dance, Psychology, and English, and studied movement therapy at UCLA. Ann has given a life of civic and community service in various organizations and boards locally and globally. She lives by the motto, "If it's God's work, I'll do it." Ann is a professional seamstress, tailor, and dressmaker, leading textile classes and sewing camps for all ages and levels.
