Mika Inouye

Mika Inouye is a performing artist and educator in Durango, Colorado. She is member of 20MOONS Dance Theatre, founding president of Four Corners Orff Chapter, and the instructor of graduate movement and music training at the Los Angeles County Orff Course. She presents Orff-Schulwerk workshops nationally and internationally, teaches K-6 music and movement, and performs as a solo and collaborative pianist in the Four Corners region and with Inouye Six Hands.
